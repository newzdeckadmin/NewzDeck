NewzDeck v3.6.93 update notes

Installed build:
- If Microsoft Defender blocks the in-app updater on v3.6.91 or another older affected build, download and run NewzDeck_v3.6.93_Setup.exe manually for this one upgrade.
- v3.6.93 carries the v3.6.92 direct verified-Setup updater and folder-only Picker architecture forward unchanged.
- The v3.6.92 source/tag remains immutable; v3.6.93 is the release-pipeline recovery roll-forward after the v3.6.92 runtime smoke test used a retired Picker mode.

Portable build:
- Close the portable NewzDeck UI/tray, replace the application files with the v3.6.93 Portable ZIP contents, then restart NewzDeck.

Persistent data remains under %LOCALAPPDATA%\NewzDeck and is not removed by a normal update.

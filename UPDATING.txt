NewzDeck v3.6.96 update notes

This release is a narrow Automation library sorting update built on the proven v3.6.95 production baseline.

After updating, TV Shows and Movies remain displayed with their canonical titles, but alphabetic placement ignores a leading A, An, or The. No library folders, media filenames, monitoring state, quality profiles, downloads, or themes are renamed or migrated.

The normal in-app verified Setup update path is unchanged.

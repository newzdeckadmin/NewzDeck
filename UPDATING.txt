NewzDeck v3.6.53
NZB Identity Gate & Durable Downloads History

INSTALLED NEWZDECK
Install the v3.6.53 Setup EXE normally. Setup owns the service/tray handoff; no manual service stop is required.

The existing NewzDeck job ledger is preserved. On first v3.6.53 startup NewzDeck bootstraps `sab-engine\terminal-history.json` from terminal records already present in the durable ledger. It is compact presentation/audit metadata only; SAB remains authoritative for live queue and post-processing state.

Automatic TV grabs now inspect actual NZB file-subject identity before SAB submission. Positive contradictory evidence (for example a single-episode target whose NZB contains many SxxExx identities or Complete Season markers) is rejected before download. Obfuscated NZBs without explicit contradictory evidence are not rejected.

The terminal-history index retains the newest 5,000 terminal rows. Removing a terminal job removes its row; retrying a failed job removes/supersedes the old row until the job becomes terminal again.

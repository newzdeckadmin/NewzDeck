NewzDeck v3.7.7 update notes

v3.7.7 is a focused hotfix for the v3.7.6 Newsgroups splitter. It corrects CSS cascade conflicts that moved the divider to the bottom of the Newsgroups pane and prevented --groups-pane-width from controlling the live workspace grid.

Existing application data and the locally saved Newsgroups pane width are preserved. No NNTP, Related Media, download, Automation, Smart Import, Backup & Restore, theme, or managed-updater behavior is intentionally changed.

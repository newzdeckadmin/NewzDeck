NewzDeck v3.7.30 update notes

On startup NewzDeck provisions the official SABnzbd 5.1.3 Windows x64 portable ZIP, verifies its SHA-256 and gracefully transfers control from an authenticated older SABnzbd engine. The upgrade is deferred without forced process termination if provisioning/authentication/shutdown fails. A temporary transfer interruption is possible. No user data or library migration is performed.
